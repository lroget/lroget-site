ArgentiNat

Argentina's node of iNaturalist, a citizen science platform developed within Fundación Vida Silvestre Argentina.

What it is

iNaturalist is a citizen science platform: anyone uploads a photo of what they see and the community identifies it. ArgentiNat is the Argentine version, with its own community and projects.

My part

I launched ArgentiNat in November 2019 and led it. It was developed within Fundación Vida Silvestre Argentina, with a grant from the National Geographic Society. I have been a National Geographic Explorer since 2019.
