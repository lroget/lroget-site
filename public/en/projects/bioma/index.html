The idea

Bringing nature closer to people who love art, and art closer to people who love nature.

Bioma is an art project I co-created with Juan Elizalde and Gonzalo Roget. We are interested in the artistic and graphic representation of nature, and wanted to move it away from hyperrealism.

Each poster is illustrated by an artist from Argentina and represents one ecoregion. We screen-print 50 copies of each, hand-printed. It is a way of standing up for the handmade.
