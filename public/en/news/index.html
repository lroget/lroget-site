Timeline

2026

  • Jul 2026PressWinter Safari 2026A national ArgentiNat campaign in which participants across the country record the biodiversity they find, developed within Fundación Vida Silvestre Argentina. I coordinated it. It was covered by Radio Nacional and agentar.info.

2025

  • 22 Feb 2025WorkshopWorkshop for EcoFam 2.0 volunteersPresentation and workshop in Arenas Verdes, Necochea, for volunteers of EcoFam 2.0, a citizen science program of Aves Argentinas (a bird conservation organization) that records stranded wildlife on the Buenos Aires coast.

2024

  • Mar 2024TalkEarth HourPresenter at "Connecting with Earth: One Planet, Many Voices", a WWF staff event ahead of the campaign. Also a citizen science session in a global education webinar (22 March) and a nature interpretation walk in Vicente López (23 March).
  • Dec 2024AgreementAgreement with Fundación Natura ArgentinaI drafted and managed the cooperation agreement between Fundación Vida Silvestre Argentina and Fundación Natura Argentina to promote the use of iNaturalist and ArgentiNat in Natura's projects. Term: 26 November 2024 to 26 November 2026. Signed by both parties in December 2024.
  • 17 Dec 2024WorkshopVirtual workshop on protected-area management plansThree-hour workshop organized within a project of the Buenos Aires Province Ministry of Environment. I presented ArgentiNat alongside other citizen science platforms.
  • Nov 2024TalkYurumí 2024Annual event of Fundación Vida Silvestre Argentina. I coordinated the panel "Contemplar la naturaleza" (Contemplating nature).
  • 16 Oct 2024WorkshopDay with teachers from ZárateIn-person day at the Toyota Reserve with 41 teachers from 36 schools in Zárate (figure reported by Toyota). I coordinated it with the Foundation's Education team.
  • 9 Oct 2024WorkshopVirtual workshop "Citizen science for teachers"Free one-hour workshop for teachers across the country, supported by Toyota Argentina. I designed the content and delivered it.
  • 25 Sep – 1 Oct 2024MilestoneArgentiNat tour through Tucumán and CatamarcaTrip with talks and workshops, funded by a citizen science grant from the Ministry of Science, Technology and Innovation, administered by the Museo Argentino de Ciencias Naturales (MACN). I organized and carried it out.
  • 25 Sep 2024TalkSeminar Series, Fundación Miguel LilloTucumán. Talk on ArgentiNat and citizen science in the seminar series of Fundación Miguel Lillo, a natural sciences research institution.
