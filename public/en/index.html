About

I work in citizen science and socio-environmental education. I have given around 50 trainings across Argentina to scientists, students, volunteers and park rangers.

I launched and led ArgentiNat, Argentina's iNaturalist node, a citizen science platform developed within Fundación Vida Silvestre Argentina.

In 2011 I founded COA Carancho, a group of volunteers who watch birds in Palermo, Buenos Aires.

I am a co-creator of Bioma, an art project: screen-printed posters of Argentina's ecoregions.

Projects

  • ArgentiNat

    Argentina's node of iNaturalist, a citizen science platform: anyone records what they see and the community identifies it. I launched it in November 2019 and led it. It was developed within Fundación Vida Silvestre Argentina.

    See the project

  • COA Carancho

    A COA is a bird-watching club. This one is a group of volunteers who watch birds in Palermo, Buenos Aires, and also plant native trees, run bird censuses and give talks. I founded it in 2011 and coordinated it from 2013 to 2015.

    See the project

  • Bioma

    An art project I started with Juan Elizalde and my brother, Gonzalo Roget. We are interested in artistic and graphic representations of nature, and we wanted to move away from hyperrealism. Each poster is illustrated by an artist from Argentina and depicts one ecoregion. We screen-print 50 copies of each, printed by hand. It is a way of standing up for the handmade.

    See the shop

  • Dipnoo

    Socio-environmental education services, in person in the Buenos Aires metropolitan area and online.
